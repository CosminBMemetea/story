Privacy Policy

Introduction

Nomad Nexus is designed so that your photos stay on your device. This page explains exactly what happens to your data.

What we process

When you add a photo, your browser reads its GPS metadata (EXIF) and looks up the matching country using a map dataset bundled with the app. The photo itself, its metadata and its location are never uploaded to a server.

What we store

Nothing. The list of detected countries lives in your browser's memory and disappears when you refresh or close the page. We use no cookies, analytics or accounts.

Third parties

The demo is a static site (GitHub Pages) and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may log standard request data such as your IP address, as described in their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are visible in the project's public Git history.
